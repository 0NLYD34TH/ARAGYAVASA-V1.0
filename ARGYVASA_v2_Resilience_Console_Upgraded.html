Risk assessment per zone

Score = 0.5 × hazard (Brain’s estimate) + 0.2 × evidence uncertainty (age ÷ 10 ticks) + 0.1 × confidence gap + 0.2 × no reachable idle interceptor. LOW below 0.35, MODERATE 0.35 to 0.59, HIGH 0.60 and above. Stale or unknown evidence is never rated LOW. Security-unit dispatch is simulated and requires human approval. Node stop/restart controls are manual test controls. The weights are demo settings, not validated values.
